## OmniNode Hub — Font Placeholder

Inter font family files must be downloaded from Google Fonts:
  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter

Download the following TTF/OTF variants and place them in:
  app/src/main/res/font/

Required files:
  inter_regular.ttf     (Weight 400)
  inter_medium.ttf      (Weight 500)
  inter_semibold.ttf    (Weight 600)
  inter_bold.ttf        (Weight 700)
  inter_extrabold.ttf   (Weight 800)

Quick download via curl (run in app/src/main/res/font/):
  curl -Lo inter_regular.ttf "https://github.com/rsms/inter/raw/master/docs/font-files/Inter-Regular.ttf"
  curl -Lo inter_medium.ttf "https://github.com/rsms/inter/raw/master/docs/font-files/Inter-Medium.ttf"
  curl -Lo inter_semibold.ttf "https://github.com/rsms/inter/raw/master/docs/font-files/Inter-SemiBold.ttf"
  curl -Lo inter_bold.ttf "https://github.com/rsms/inter/raw/master/docs/font-files/Inter-Bold.ttf"
  curl -Lo inter_extrabold.ttf "https://github.com/rsms/inter/raw/master/docs/font-files/Inter-ExtraBold.ttf"
